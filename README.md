Architects' Contingency Manual
Section I: Foundations & Post-Event System Principles
1. Primary Operating Principles
 * Energy Independence First: Physical and administrative autonomy depends strictly on decentralized power and closed-loop infrastructure before scaling population or complexity.
 * Modular Scalability: Hardware, architectural blueprints, and societal frameworks must be modular, repairable, and built with local materials.
 * Decentralized Information Preservation: Critical technical knowledge must exist in offline, hardened, redundant formats accessible without centralized network backbones.
Section II: Historic Restart Models, Failure Modes & Hazards
| Model / Approach | Historic / Theoretical Blueprint | Failure Modes & Primary Hazards | Technical & Systemic Reason for Revision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Centralized Resource Monopolization | Command-and-control distribution of energy, seed stock, and water (e.g., early agrarian state models, centralized command centers). | Single point of failure; vulnerability to localized sabotage; corruption of central administration; extreme friction during supply line disruption. | Replaced by Decentralized Node Networks. Distribution and production must occur at localized perimeter nodes to prevent single-point collapse. |
| Technological Hyper-Leap Strategy | Attempting to immediately rebuild complex high-tier infrastructure (e.g., silicon fabs, complex combustion grids) without intermediate industrial steps. | Total resource exhaustion; rapid depletion of non-renewable spares; severe loss of fundamental operational knowledge when advanced tech fails. | Replaced by Staged Technology Tiering. Systems must be built on low-complexity, high-durability infrastructure (e.g., 48V DC solar grids, modular mechanical hand tools) before attempting higher-order complexity. |
| Unconstrained Expansion without Closed-Loop Systems | Rapid land acquisition and population growth without localized waste-to-resource cycles. | Resource depletion, soil degradation, water table contamination, and logistics failure over long lines of communication. | Replaced by Site-Adaptive Living Models. Operational perimeters are strictly limited by local carrying capacity, closed-loop permaculture, and localized energy production. |
| Single-Repository Knowledge Storage | Storing critical instructions, engineering schematics, and medical manuals in a single archive location or fragile digital medium. | Total loss of technical knowledge due to localized disaster, hardware degradation, or access loss over generations. | Replaced by Redundant Sovereign Archives. Knowledge must be multi-formatted, distributed across living units, and integrated into practical, daily operational frameworks. |

Phase 0: Immediate Survival & Autonomy Grounding
 * Localized Power Grid: Deploy 48V DC nominal off-grid solar and battery storage backbone to support baseline systems, communications, and telemetry.
 * Sovereign Water & Sheltering: Secure immediate access to reliable water sources, filtration infrastructure, and hardened dwelling perimeters.
 * Offline Knowledge Vault: Initialize access to local technical archives, operational logs, and essential engineering documentation.
Phase 1: Operational Base & Resource Cycles
 * Modular Production Tools: Establish base workshop capabilities for local repair, handtool fabrication, and structural maintenance.
 * Closed-Loop Agricultural Units: Implement sustainable, high-yield soil building, balcony/indoor cultivation, and localized seed saving.
 * Secure Regional Communications: Set up short-range and line-of-sight radio infrastructure powered independently of municipal grids.
Phase 2: System Expansion & Knowledge Continuity
 * Modular Apparel & Gear Deployment: Produce utility gear, attachment bands, and environmental protection equipment built for high friction/wear operations.
 * Standardized System Licensing & Protocols: Establish governance protocols, system architecture frameworks, and clear continuity instructions for successor operators.
